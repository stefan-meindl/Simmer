I won't be home in time to cook dinner today and so i cooked before breakfast instead. This is an improvized chicken curry with "white masala". Or it might be more accurate to say it's a variation on other recipes and improvizations.

Whole garam masala is made fragrant and dried red chillies are puffed up in ghee; then sliced red onions go in and are cooked on low heat till soft and sweet. A whole head's worth of garlic cloves go in next (the cloves were left whole). I let the garlic brown a bit and then added the chicken (a whole chicken cut into 10 pieces) and salt and sauteed it all for about 10 minutes.

The ground masala went in next (1 tblspn poppy seeds, 1 tblspn sesame seeds, 1 tblspn coriander seeds, 1 tblspn fennel seeds, 1 tspn methi/fenugreek seeds: ground together) along with one tblspn powdered ginger. I mixed it all in and sauteed for a few more minutes. Then I crumbled and mixed the kasuri methi in.

Then 2 cups of water, fresh off the boil, a good stir and it was cooked, covered, over low heat till the chicken was done. We will eat it tonight with steamed rice and tomorrow with parathas.
