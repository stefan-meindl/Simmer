Grandma's Banana Bread

Makes 1 loaf (9x5 inch pan)

Ingredients:
- 3 ripe bananas, mashed
- 1/3 cup (5 tbsp + 1 tsp) melted butter
- 3/4 cup sugar
- 1 egg, beaten
- 1 tsp vanilla
- 1 tsp baking soda
- Pinch of salt
- 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
- 1/2 cup chopped walnuts (optional)

Directions:
Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Butter a 9x5 inch loaf pan.
Mix the mashed bananas into the melted butter. Stir in sugar, egg and vanilla. Sprinkle baking soda and salt over the mixture and mix in. Add the flour last, and the walnuts if using.
Pour into the loaf pan and bake for 55 to 60 minutes, until a toothpick comes out clean. Cool in the pan for 10 minutes, then turn out onto a wire rack.
