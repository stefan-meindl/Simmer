Greek salad

4 ripe tomatoes
1 cucumber
1 red onion
200 g feta
a handful of kalamata olives
4 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
1 tbsp red wine vinegar
dried oregano

Chop tomatoes and cucumber into chunks, slice the onion thinly. Combine in a bowl with olives. Top with a slab of feta. Dress with oil, vinegar and oregano. Serve right away.
