Easy Weeknight Shakshuka (Ready in 25 Minutes!)

Jump to Recipe

I first had shakshuka on a rainy morning in Tel Aviv, sitting at a tiny table outside a café that has since closed. Since then I have made it more times than I can count, for brunch, for dinner, even for a picnic once (don't ask). If you have never tried it, you are in for a treat. It is cheap, it is fast, and it is one of those dishes that makes people think you tried much harder than you did. Some readers have asked about swapping the feta for halloumi, and honestly that works too.

Prep time: 10 mins
Cook time: 15 mins
Serves 4

Ingredients
2 tbsp olive oil
1 large onion, diced
1 red pepper, diced
3 cloves garlic, minced
2 tsp ground cumin
1 tsp smoked paprika
1 tin (400 g) chopped tomatoes
1 tin (400 g) passata
6 eggs
100 g feta, crumbled
Fresh coriander, to serve
Chilli flakes, optional

Instructions
Heat the oil in a large deep frying pan with a lid. Fry the onion and pepper for 6-7 minutes until soft.
Add the garlic, cumin and paprika and cook for 1 minute.
Pour in the tomatoes and passata, season and simmer for 10 minutes until thick.
Make 6 wells in the sauce and crack in the eggs. Cover and cook for 5-6 minutes until the whites are set.
Top with feta and coriander and serve with bread.
