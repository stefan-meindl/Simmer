Weeknight Beef Chilli

Serves 6, about 1 hour 15 minutes

You need:
1 tbsp vegetable oil
1 onion, chopped
2 garlic cloves, crushed
500g beef mince
2 tsp chilli powder
1 tsp ground cumin
1 tin chopped tomatoes (400g)
1 tin kidney beans, drained
250 ml beef stock
1 square dark chocolate (optional)
Sour cream and grated cheese, to serve (optional)
1 jalapeño, sliced (optional, for heat)

Fry the onion in the oil until soft, about 8 minutes. Add garlic and mince and brown well. Stir in the spices, tomatoes, beans and stock. Simmer gently for 45 minutes, stirring now and then. Stir in the chocolate if using. Taste and season. Serve with sour cream, cheese and jalapeño.
