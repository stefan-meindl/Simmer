Japanese-style rice cooker chicken rice

Serves 3
Total time: 55 min

300 g jasmine rice, rinsed
400 ml chicken stock
3 chicken thighs, boneless
2 tbsp soy sauce
1 tbsp mirin
1 tsp grated ginger
2 spring onions, sliced

Marinate the chicken in the soy, mirin and ginger for 15 minutes. Put the rice and stock in the rice cooker bowl, lay the chicken on top and start the normal cooking cycle (about 30 minutes). Let it rest 10 minutes with the lid closed, then slice the chicken, fluff the rice and top with spring onions.
If you have no rice cooker, cook it in a covered pot on low heat for 18 minutes.
