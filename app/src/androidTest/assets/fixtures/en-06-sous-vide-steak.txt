Sous vide ribeye steak

2 ribeye steaks, 2.5 cm thick
2 tbsp butter
3 sprigs thyme
2 cloves garlic, smashed
Salt and black pepper

Set the sous vide circulator to 54°C. Season the steaks and seal them in bags with the thyme and garlic. Cook in the water bath for 1 to 3 hours. Pat dry. Sear in a very hot pan with the butter for 45 seconds a side. Rest for 3 minutes.
