Crispy chicken wings (oven or air fryer)

Serves 4 as a snack

900g chicken wings, split
1 tbsp baking powder (aluminium free)
1 tsp smoked paprika
1 tsp garlic powder
1 tsp salt
3 tbsp hot sauce
2 tbsp butter, melted

Dry the wings well and toss with baking powder, paprika, garlic powder and salt.
Oven: bake on a rack at 220°C for 45-50 minutes, turning halfway.
Air fryer: cook at 200°C for 22-25 minutes, shaking the basket halfway.
Mix hot sauce and butter and toss the hot wings in it.
