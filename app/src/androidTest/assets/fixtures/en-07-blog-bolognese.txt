The Only Bolognese Recipe You'll Ever Need

By Jenny | Updated March 2023 | 4.9 from 212 votes

Let me tell you a story. When I was twenty-two I moved to Bologna for six months and lived above a tiny trattoria run by a woman called Nonna Pina. She never wrote anything down. Everything was by eye and by smell. When I left she pressed a stained piece of paper into my hand, and this is my best attempt at it. Print it, pin it, make it on a Sunday. Please, please do not add garlic. I will not be taking questions.

Ingredients
1 onion, finely chopped
1 carrot, finely chopped
1 stick celery, finely chopped
2 tbsp olive oil
500 g beef mince
150 g pancetta, diced
200 ml red wine
2 tbsp tomato purée
400 g tinned tomatoes
250 ml whole milk
400 g tagliatelle
Parmesan, to serve

Method
Soften the onion, carrot and celery in the oil over a low heat for 10 minutes. Add the pancetta and fry until the fat runs, then add the mince and brown it well.
Pour in the wine and let it bubble until almost gone. Stir in the purée, tomatoes and milk. Cover partly and simmer on the lowest heat for 2 hours, stirring occasionally.
Cook the tagliatelle in salted boiling water according to the packet. Toss with the sauce and serve with Parmesan.

Nutrition per serving: 720 kcal, 38 g protein.
