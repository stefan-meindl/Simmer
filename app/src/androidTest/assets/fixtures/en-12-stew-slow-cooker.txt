Slow cooker beef stew

Serves 6 | Prep 20 min | Cook 8 hours on low

1 kg stewing beef, cubed
3 tbsp flour
2 tbsp oil
4 carrots, chunked
500 g potatoes, cubed
1 onion, sliced
500 ml beef stock
2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
2 bay leaves
Frozen peas, a handful (optional)

Toss the beef in flour and brown in the oil in batches. Put everything except the peas in the slow cooker. Cook on low for 8 hours, or on high for 4-5 hours. Stir in the peas for the last 15 minutes.
Stovetop option: simmer covered in a heavy pot for 2 1/2 hours.
