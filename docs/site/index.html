Simmer: open-source Android recipe app

Import a recipe from an Instagram Reel, set servings, pick variations, and cook step by step with timers. Runs on your phone, with on-device AI and no account.

Download the APK

What Simmer does

  • Import from Instagram Reels

    Share a Reel to Simmer to get its caption, or press Listen to turn the sound into text on your phone with Whisper.

  • Chatbot import with a Fix prompt

    Open the recipe in Gemini, paste the reply back, and copy a Fix prompt when a paste does not parse.

  • Servings and variations

    Scale amounts to the number of people and pick swaps and add-ins. Steps follow your choices.

  • Cooking with timers

    Work through one step at a time. Timers run as notifications with Pause, Resume and Reset.

  • Shopping list

    Planned recipes add their ingredients, grouped by aisle. Clear ticked items or the whole list, with Undo.

  • Swipe to unplan or stop

    Swipe a Planned or Cooking now card away, and bring it back with Undo.

  • Your kitchen

    Pick your appliances so new recipes only use what you have.

  • Edit recipes

    Change any recipe, and restore the original of a built-in one.

  • On-device AI, no account

    Nothing to sign up for. Transcription runs locally on your phone.

  • Open source

    MIT licensed. Read the code or file an issue on GitHub.

Install

Simmer is installed by sideloading the APK. It needs Android 8.0 or newer.

  1. Download the APK from the releases page.
  2. Allow "install unknown apps" for your browser or file manager, then open the APK.
